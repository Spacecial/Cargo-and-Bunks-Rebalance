# Cargo & Bunks Rebalance
Is your favorite ship have too much cargo space but not enough bunks for capturing missions? Or it has so many bunks that's too expensive for just one-click 'hire all' the extra crew and too much hassle for hire them one-by-one? You want to readjust their capacity without sacrificing your precious outfit space? Then this plugin is for you!

This plugin add two new outfits for rebalancing the cargo space and bunk space without taking up the outfit space of your ships.
